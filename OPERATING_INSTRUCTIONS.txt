================================================================================
  MAZE BALL GAME - Operating Instructions
  PIC24FJ256GA705 Curiosity board + OLED C Click + Accel (ADXL345) Click
================================================================================

GOAL
----
Tilt the board to roll the red ball from its start at the bottom of the maze
onto the green door in the center before the timer runs out. Finishing
faster gives a better (lower) score. Gold coins are scattered along the
corridors - collect them on the way for bragging rights (the score is still
the time; the counter top-right and the end screens show how many you got).

HARDWARE SETUP
--------------
  * OLED C Click  -> mikroBUS socket A (left).
  * Accel Click   -> mikroBUS socket B (right).
  * Connect the board over USB and program it (MPLAB X -> Make and Program).
  * Hold the board flat to start; the ball is still when the board is level.
  * If an "I2C ERR" screen appears when starting a game, re-seat the Accel
    Click board and press a key to try again.

CONTROLS
--------
  * Potentiometer .... scrolls menu options / picks a letter when entering a name.
  * Button S1 ........ select / move name cursor LEFT.
  * Button S2 ........ select / move name cursor RIGHT.
  * S1 + S2 together . save the name (in the name-entry screen).
  * Short press S1/S2  during a game: pause (press again to resume; the
                       clock stops while paused).
  * Hold S1 for 2 s .. during a game, returns to the main menu (ends the game).

MAIN MENU (shown at power-up)
-----------------------------
Turn the potentiometer to highlight an option, then press S1 or S2 to
choose it:

  LEVEL n  - press to cycle the difficulty 1 -> 2 -> 3:
                 Level 1 : 40.0 s, slow ball  (speed factor 0.8)
                 Level 2 : 30.0 s, normal     (speed factor 1.0)
                 Level 3 : 20.0 s, fast ball  (speed factor 1.2)
  START    - begin a game at the chosen difficulty.
  SCORES   - show the "Big Three" best times (kept until power-off).
  HELP     - a quick reminder of the controls, on the screen itself.

PLAYING
-------
  * A short 3-2-1 countdown runs before the ball unlocks - level the board.
  * The remaining time is shown top-left in tenths of a second (e.g. 12.3).
  * Tilt the board: the steeper the tilt, the faster the ball rolls. Movement
    is separate for the X and Y axes, matching the maze corridors.
  * The ball cannot pass through walls or leave the screen.
  * In the LAST 5 SECONDS the screen blinks (normal <-> inverse, twice a second)
    to warn you to hurry.

END OF A GAME
-------------
  * TIME RAN OUT: a "Next time..." screen appears. Press any key to return to
    the menu.
  * YOU REACHED THE FINISH: a "WELL DONE!" screen appears. Press any key to see
    your score (your time, lower is better), your coin count, and NEW RECORD!
    if you beat the best time.
        - If your time makes the Big Three, you are asked to enter your name:
              turn the potentiometer to pick each letter (A..Z),
              S1 / S2 move the cursor left / right,
              hold S1 + S2 together briefly to save,
              or hold S1 alone to skip without saving.
        - If it does not qualify, just press a key to return to the menu.

TIPS
----
  * Small, gentle tilts give fine control; large tilts are fast but easy to
    overshoot. Start on Level 1 to learn the maze.
================================================================================
